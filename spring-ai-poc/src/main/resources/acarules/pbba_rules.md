## About BBA Centralized Partnership Audit Regime (Often referred to as PBBA)
The Partnership Bipartisan Budget Act (PBBA), or more commonly the BBA Centralized Partnership Audit Regime (CPAR), is an IRS framework for auditing partnerships. 
It replaced the old TEFRA rules, allowing the IRS to audit partnerships at the entity level rather than at the individual partner level.


## Who can use the PBBA system
- Audited BBA partnerships or their pass-through partners 





## New filers
If you’re a new filer, you must apply for a Transmitter Control Code (TCC) to electronically submit forms to PBBA   

## Forms  
Valid form types for pbba are: **FORM8980, FORM8981, FORM8985, FORM8988, FORM8989, FORM8984, FORM14027.**   

Only provide information about submission made after January 1, 2016.
