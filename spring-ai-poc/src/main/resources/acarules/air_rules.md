## About ACA Information Return reporting (Often referred to as AIR)  
Under the Affordable Care Act (ACA), insurance companies, self-insured companies and businesses that provide health insurance 
to their employees must submit information returns to the IRS to report on individual health insurance coverage. 
If you have 10 or more information returns, you must submit them electronically.

## Who can use the AIR system
- Employers
- Software developers
- Insurance issuers or carriers
- Government agencies
- Third parties that submit returns for their own company or other companies  

## New filers
If you’re a new filer, you must apply for an ACA Transmitter Control Code (TCC) before you can use AIR.  

## Forms
Valid form types for air are: **FORM1095A, FORM1095B, FORM1095C, FORM1094B, FORM1094C.**  

Only provide information about submission made after January 1, 2016.
